# Afandem is starting

The app opens in a new browser tab by itself once it is running (about a minute after the Codespace starts).

If no tab opened:

1. Click the **Ports** tab at the bottom of this window.
2. On the row **Afandem (8000)**, click the globe icon ("Open in Browser").

Then click **Play demo** in the app's sidebar. With no key it replays a recorded run; for live chat, add a
free Gemini key as the Codespace secret `GEMINI_API_KEY` and restart the Codespace.

If the Ports tab is empty, open a terminal and run `bash .devcontainer/start.sh`.
